01Why this exists

Fraud desks forget.
Every week, they start over.

01

Work gets repeated

An analyst spends an hour on a case, closes it — and the same pattern walks in next week. Nobody remembers.

02

Lessons are lost

The reason behind a decision is written somewhere, but nothing connects it to the next similar case.

03

Wrong freezes stick for months

An honest business gets frozen. The mistake repeats, because the system remembers the freeze but not why it was wrong.

Real banks have years of case history. Almost none of it is searchable, citable, or teachable to the next investigator. That is the gap OmegaDesk fills.

02How it works

Five steps, from a payment
to a lesson the desk keeps.

  1. 01

    A watcher with plain maths

    fixed rules

    Every moment, fixed rules look at the money: one account paid by many? money leaving instantly? payments hiding under the reporting line? one device on many accounts?

  2. 02

    A case file, not one suspect

    fixed rules

    The desk captures the whole crowd — receivers, payers, shared devices — because the fraud shape lives in the relationships.

  3. 03

    An AI detective

    AI

    An agent reads the file, asks the graph questions one by one, and reads the desk's memory of past cases. It answers with a verdict, a certainty score, and the past cases it leaned on.

  4. 04

    Rules make the final call

    rule ladder

    A fixed rule ladder converts the verdict into action — freeze, human review, or watch. The AI can never release an account without fresh proof run that day.

    freezehuman reviewwatch
  5. 05

    Humans correct — and the desk learns

    human

    An analyst who disagrees leaves a written reason. That reason becomes "case law": future similar cases cite it, and the old wrong decision is marked never-cite-again.

03The memory idea

Same shape.
Different outcome.

Without memory

A look-alike of a merchant the desk had already cleared gets frozen. Apology letter pending.

frozen
With memory

The same case cites the earlier clearance, runs its own fresh proof, and clears it legally.

cleared, with proof

Same shape. Different outcome. The desk remembered.

What you decide, and why you corrected us, becomes law — searchable, citable, impossible to silently forget.
