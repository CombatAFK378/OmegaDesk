01A day at the desk

Money walks through the door.
Here is everyone who touches it.

  1. 01

    The world

    simulator

    A simulated bank runs on its own. People open accounts, use devices, buy from merchants and move money. Realistic fraud shapes are planted inside the ordinary traffic.

  2. 02

    The watcher

    fixed rules

    Every few moments, simple maths checks the money. Did one account receive from many people? Did money leave the moment it arrived? Are payments hiding just under the reporting line? Is one device shared across accounts? When a shape is strong enough, an alert appears in plain words.

  3. 03

    The case file

    fixed rules

    Investigating opens a case: the suspect account plus everyone around it in that time window, senders and receivers. The pattern lives in the shape of the crowd, not in one account alone.

  4. 04

    The measuring

    fixed rules

    Still no AI. The desk writes down what it measured, one tag per pattern, each marked as guilty-looking or innocent-looking.

    many pay oneunder the lineone device pass-throughknown business
  5. 05

    The AI investigator

    AI

    The AI's only job. It reads the file and asks the graph questions one at a time: who else shares this device, where did the money go next, are these accounts new? It reads the desk's memory of past cases and human corrections, decides which old cases really match, then answers in a fixed format.

    verdictfraud · legit · unclear
    confidencebold or humble, by rule
    cases it leaned onnamed, one by one
  6. 06

    The guard

    code

    Fixed rules turn the verdict into an action. There are two hard guard rails. The AI may never clear an account just because an old case did; clearing needs fresh proof. And to disagree with a corrected old case, it must name exactly what is different today.

  7. 07

    The receipt

    code

    When the action is a clearance, a tool proves the clean look. A checklist of checks all come back clean, and the result is sealed to the decision like a signed slip that can be re-run at any time.

  8. 08

    The human

    human

    Real cases land with the right analyst: device-ring cases go to one desk, business cases to another. An analyst who disagrees can override, but only with a written reason.

  9. 09

    The lesson

    human

    That reason is saved as a correction. Future similar cases cite it like case law, and the old precedent it beat is marked "never cite again". Memory only grows through decisions, never through silence.

  10. 10

    The written record

    AI

    Any closed case becomes a readable report for a supervisor. Every sentence carries its source: the decision, the measurements, the receipt and the past cases.

  11. 11

    The proof

    code

    A built-in test replays six known cases twice, once with memory and once without, and shows which decisions changed. Then the desk keeps working live as new alerts arrive on their own.
